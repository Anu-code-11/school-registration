About our school

Wonji Didimtu Secondary School serves students from Wonji and the surrounding community.

Our mission

To give every student a strong academic foundation, good character, and the confidence to continue to higher education or skilled work, and to serve their community and Ethiopia.

What we offer

  • General secondary education, Grades 9 and 10
  • Preparatory education, Grades 11 and 12
  • Natural Science and Social Science streams
  • Guidance and support from qualified teachers

Registration requirements

Bring these documents to the school office to complete your registration.

Documents to bring

  • Grade 8 certificate (for Grade 9) or Grade 10 certificate (for Grades 11–12)
  • Transcript or last report card
  • 2 recent passport-size photos
  • Transfer letter from your previous school (transfer students)
  • Photo ID of the student, or a kebele ID of the guardian

Before you apply

  • Use a phone number the school can call
  • Write your name as it appears on your certificate
  • Grade 11–12 applicants choose a stream: Natural Science or Social Science
  • Keep your reference number after you submit
